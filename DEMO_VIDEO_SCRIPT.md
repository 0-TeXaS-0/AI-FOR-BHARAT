- Terminal: Frontend running on port 3000
- Browser DevTools: Show smooth animations (500ms transitions)

### Voice-over Tips:
- **Pace**: Conversational but energetic (150-160 words/minute)
- **Emphasis**: Highlight "AI-powered," "bilingual," "real-time translation"
- **Tone**: Excited about features, professional about technical aspects
- **Language**: Mix English with occasional Hindi terms (टमाटर, मंडी)

### Screen Recording Settings:
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (1080p)
- **Frame Rate**: 60fps for smooth animations
- **Cursor**: Show during interaction, hide during transitions
- **Browser**: Chrome (enable DevTools for network/console shots)
- **Audio**: Clear voiceover + subtle background music

### Background Music Suggestions:
- Light, modern tech music (120-130 BPM)
- Indian instrumental fusion (low volume, 15-20%)
- Upbeat but not distracting
- Fade in/out at beginning and end

### Transitions:
- Use smooth fade/slide transitions (300-500ms)
- Match the app's animation speed
- Keep cuts clean and purposeful

### Text Overlays:
- Feature names in **bold white** with subtle shadow
- Stats/numbers in **orange accent color** (#FF851B)
- Use app's font family (Inter/system fonts)
